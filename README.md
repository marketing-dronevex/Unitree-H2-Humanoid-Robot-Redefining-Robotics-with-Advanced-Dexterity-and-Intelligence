The Unitree H2 is a next-generation full-sized humanoid robot designed for versatility, precision, and interaction. Standing at approximately 180 cm and weighing 70 kg, the H2 combines human-like articulation with robust computational power, making it ideal for research, service, and interactive applications. Its sleek frame is engineered with advanced motors delivering high joint torque and precision control, enabling it to perform complex movements such as dancing, object manipulation, and agile locomotion with unmatched grace.

## Key Features

- **31 Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** Enhanced limb articulation allows for fluid, natural motion across arms, legs, waist, and neck.  
- **Dexterous Manipulation:** Both arms have full 7 DOF with payload capacity up to 7 kg (rated) and 21 kg peak, ideal for complex manipulation tasks.  
- **Human-like Interaction:** Features a bionic face with animated expressions, binocular vision system, and voice interaction capabilities.  
- **Robust Computing Platform:** Powered by Intel i5/i7 processors coupled with up to three NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX modules for AI and autonomous decision-making.  
- **Advanced Mobility:** Hip and waist designed for better balance and flexibility, enabling stable walking and dynamic movement over variable terrain.  
- **Extended Operation:** Runs on a 15 Ah lithium battery providing approximately 3 hours of operation, with Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.2 for connectivity.
